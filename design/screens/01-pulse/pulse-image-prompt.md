# Pulse image prompt

Use if a still must be generated. The HTML prototype is the source of truth.

## Prompt

Dark operational control room, not a SaaS dashboard. 1440×1024. Near-black canvas `#080a0d` with a quiet left rail and a right inspector. No purple gradients, no glassmorphism, no giant rounded cards, no charts.

Left rail: “EvoPulse” in serif, five sparse uppercase items — PULSE (active, thin orange hairline) COMMAND TIMELINE BUSINESS GOALS.

Center: large serif headline “Your business is running.” Under it, three typographic counts, not tiles: “2 need you” (orange numeral) / “0 monitoring” / “0 handled automatically.”

Attention list as hairline rows, not cards:

1. NEEDS YOU · AT RISK — NOT MISSED · Atlas Supply · SH-204 Monday → Wednesday · 850,000 DZD associated revenue · 3 orders · 3 customers · 540,000 DZD expected cash timing.
2. NEEDS APPROVAL · Revised 320,000 DZD proposal never sent · Atlas Retail · Amine Khelifi.

Right inspector: quote “Your shipment will arrive Wednesday instead of Monday.” Expected Monday 28 Sep 09:00 vs actual Wednesday 30 Sep 09:00. Source, timestamp, 96% confidence. Primary action “Open cascade.” Small text “Ask EvoPulse about this…” — not a chat box.

Typography: Instrument Serif headlines, IBM Plex Sans UI, IBM Plex Mono amounts. Accent only `#f0a202`. Minimal shadow. High numeric legibility.

## Negative

CRM boards, ERP tables, BI charts, ChatGPT thread, Kanban, workflow builder, rainbow statuses, glass cards, purple AI glow, “lost revenue,” “saved revenue,” duplicate Exception + Approval + Autopilot cards for one situation.
