# Mobbin research — Control OS foundation

Phase 0 studied **current web** workspace patterns. The brief’s reference URL resolves to a **Sana AI** web screen, not ChatGPT. Both were studied, plus additional ChatGPT Search / desktop workspace screens, Claude, Linear, Notion, Attio, Raycast (iOS — no Raycast web on Mobbin), and command-palette analogs.

Do not copy branding, pixels, icons, colors, or typography.

For every reference: **what to take · what not to take · why it fits EvoPulse**.

Task intent used for all Mobbin queries: research ChatGPT-class workspace patterns for the EvoPulse Control OS shell.

## 1. ChatGPT — Search + sources (primary structural direction)

Cited screens:

- [ChatGPT search with sources sidebar](https://mobbin.com/screens/73833b79-1dd5-4354-8fc4-a2e99c33a75e)
- [ChatGPT search overlay](https://mobbin.com/screens/169c1c0e-8ed0-486e-ac7b-ba18036558d8)
- [ChatGPT activity + sources while researching](https://mobbin.com/screens/b045e2cd-4f54-424a-97f6-4c5954f0c1e1)
- [ChatGPT citations rail](https://mobbin.com/screens/88d5e839-cf2b-447b-887c-a4beeead8040)
- [ChatGPT research report + sources](https://mobbin.com/screens/1d43b453-a7b4-438f-8038-f9d5c16c903e)
- [ChatGPT empty research composer](https://mobbin.com/screens/c3f1aa21-87cc-4212-919d-7e9e8337ce5e)

### Take

- Input as navigation. The composer is how you move, not a chat toy at the bottom of a dashboard.
- Quiet left rail: few verbs, low contrast, no module circus.
- Centered readable column. Content does not kiss the viewport edge.
- Sources as a **secondary panel**, opened in context, not a permanent third app.
- Progressive disclosure: “23 sources” / “Activity” appear when there is something to inspect.
- Search overlay over the same shell (history stays visible, dimmed).

### Do not take

- OpenAI wordmark, green send disc, black pill composer.
- Chat-history-as-IA. EvoPulse is not a list of past threads.
- Web-citation chrome (favicon + publisher + URL).
- Shopping / image-result layouts.
- “What’s on your mind today?” consumer greeting.

### Why it fits

This is the strongest available pattern for **restraint + conversational entry + evidence beside an answer**. EvoPulse replaces web sources with business sources, and chat history with five operating destinations.

## 2. ChatGPT — desktop sidebar + home composer

Cited screens:

- [Project workspace + new chat in project](https://mobbin.com/screens/8b37adf6-385f-4d04-afb8-99ce66b1d17f)
- [Conversation with overflow menu](https://mobbin.com/screens/0729631b-f0f3-423c-8eff-5903dc2d0ca9)
- [Empty home “What’s on your mind”](https://mobbin.com/screens/9729b1c4-e00e-48c9-96dd-9cb6731faaac)
- [Home composer, no tabs](https://mobbin.com/screens/c8132ef1-527d-4d35-b44e-4edb1440937e)

### Take

- Sidebar density: ~32–36px rows, tiny radius, icon + label, whisper-quiet active state.
- New action at the top of the rail.
- Search is a first-class trigger, not buried in a menu.
- Home is a sentence + one composer. No KPI row.

### Do not take

- GPTs / Images / Codex / Projects as primary nav (engine names).
- User + upgrade stacked as marketing.
- Pure-white sterile canvas.

### Why it fits

Proves a deep product can hide depth. EvoPulse must go further: the rail is **Pulse / Command / Timeline / Business / Goals**, never engine names.

## 3. Sana AI — referenced screen + workspace

The brief link [mobbin.com/screens/9098947d-…](https://mobbin.com/screens/9098947d-d197-4f05-a54d-de310aa638be) is catalogued as **Sana AI**, “Chatting & Sending Messages / Searching & Finding”. Additional current Sana screens:

- [Sana home composer + suggested actions](https://mobbin.com/screens/88ffd748-498a-491f-bcd6-79fde9011ce6)
- [Sana “What would you like to do?”](https://mobbin.com/screens/1e7cc06f-cd52-45e7-8ff9-266102746d09)
- [Sana sources popover](https://mobbin.com/screens/55c17b8e-0354-4fb0-81f2-bd2b829afd50)
- [Sana search with source filters](https://mobbin.com/screens/77f9f4bc-2d72-40bd-a645-9c1260fa43e5)

### Take

- Composer as the workspace, with suggested **operational** actions under it (not trivia prompts).
- Sources as a compact disclosure on the composer, not a second product.
- Search results grouped by type (files, meetings, people-like rows).
- Extremely restrained rail: New, Workflows, Search, Meetings.

### Do not take

- SaaS upgrade card in the sidebar.
- App-connector marketing (“Connect your apps”).
- Generic “What would you like to do?” with no business stakes.
- Dark-theme clone (Sana’s dark home is still a chat product).

### Why it fits

Sana is closer to **work** than ChatGPT Search. Suggested actions map to EvoPulse’s ASK / SEARCH / ACT. We reject the connector storefront and keep suggested commands about Atlas, cash, and policy.

## 4. Claude — warm canvas, focused column

Cited screens:

- [Claude project + composer](https://mobbin.com/screens/207840d2-d842-4c07-9758-07ad8b4f50c3)
- [Claude welcome composer](https://mobbin.com/screens/e8c0af18-6e3a-4273-93c1-935e958ff8b3)
- [Claude reading column + reply composer](https://mobbin.com/screens/62554846-ab6f-4d72-b509-7389df69716e)

### Take

- Warm off-white canvas (not #FFF). This is the Aurora direction.
- Sidebar even quieter than ChatGPT.
- Centered reading measure for reasoning.
- Composer is a surface, not a black pill.

### Do not take

- Model picker as a primary control.
- Artifact / project chrome as top-level IA.
- Anthropic starburst + greeting voice.

### Why it fits

Warmth without playfulness. EvoPulse’s Aurora canvas should feel like paper on a desk, not a consumer chat void.

## 5. Linear — operational precision

Cited screens:

- [Linear issue list, quiet sidebar](https://mobbin.com/screens/9ec39891-cdbb-4d57-b0c6-de43f82c5f4e)
- [Linear grouped list + density](https://mobbin.com/screens/0ac97560-1aef-4907-a356-8c18c749437b)
- [Linear properties inspector](https://mobbin.com/screens/212fda35-366e-4dc0-a1d1-3b679659d6ab)
- [Linear in-view search](https://mobbin.com/screens/a2f96ae5-202c-4747-a959-d33d7e5c0f72)
- [Linear Ask home](https://mobbin.com/screens/12fc9c16-9010-423f-9ea7-0b5a1567880e)

### Take

- Status as a small glyph **plus a word**. Color is never the only signal.
- Right inspector for properties while the list stays put.
- Hairline, 32–36px rows, no capsules.
- Command / search as a first-class way to jump to an object.
- Ask Linear: one composer, suggested **work** cards — but keep ours as commands, not onboarding tiles.

### Do not take

- Issue-tracker IA (Inbox, Cycles, Initiatives, backlog counts).
- Filter/property explosion in the primary chrome.
- Permanent properties rail on every screen.
- “Ask Linear” as a separate product home. Command is a surface inside the OS.

### Why it fits

This is the operational half of the desired feeling: **Linear-level precision** on situations, objects, and states. Pulse is not an issue tracker; a Situation is closer to an incident than a ticket.

## 6. Notion — command + grouped search

Cited screens:

- [Notion search / ask overlay](https://mobbin.com/screens/b1273bdd-d65d-44be-a422-ffc0f601bacd)
- [Notion query with grouped results + preview](https://mobbin.com/screens/6573e834-dc7c-42e4-812f-be159ac1f887)
- [Notion focused document column](https://mobbin.com/screens/57363461-9d4f-4b99-869e-4cc8216f3b20)

### Take

- `⌘K`-class overlay that can **ask or search**.
- Results grouped; a preview pane for the highlighted hit.
- Page title as the header. No dashboard masthead.

### Do not take

- Nested page tree as the product model.
- Slash-menu everything.
- Marketplace / templates / agents storefront.
- Document-first metaphor. EvoPulse objects are live business state.

### Why it fits

Command must search **objects**, not pages. The overlay pattern and grouping are correct; the wiki IA is not.

## 7. Attio — object + inspector

Cited screens:

- [Attio company: activity + record details](https://mobbin.com/screens/d76ba56f-98c3-463f-9e1d-bbe4831ee47a)
- [Attio team + record picker](https://mobbin.com/screens/059b896a-e63b-4719-8403-bd4e7bce174d)
- [Attio empty-ish record details](https://mobbin.com/screens/1bdfc233-e1f6-4cd6-82e3-2baf285d29c9)

### Take

- One object, one shell: identity, timeline/activity, relationships, a details rail.
- Consistent record paradigm across companies and people.
- Inspector for attributes while the workspace keeps the story.

### Do not take

- CRM module sidebar (Companies, People, Lists, Reports…).
- Attribute editor as the point of the product.
- Always-on details rail.

### Why it fits

Confirms the **canonical object view**. EvoPulse objects add commitments, risks, actions, and evidence — and the inspector is on-demand, not a CRM property sheet.

## Synthesis — adopted vs rejected

### ChatGPT patterns adopted

- Restraint and whitespace
- Conversational entry as navigation
- Quiet sidebar, 32–36px rows
- Progressive disclosure
- Large readable workspace
- Contextual sources / activity panel
- Overlay search that does not destroy the shell
- Context continuity (same chrome while inspecting)

### ChatGPT patterns rejected

- Brand, color, send-button, exact composer
- Chat-history IA
- GPT / image / shopping surfaces
- Consumer greeting
- Web-citation metaphor (replaced by business evidence)
- Answer-only product character

### Also adopted (not from ChatGPT)

- Linear: status words, inspector, operational density
- Notion: ask+search overlay, grouped hits
- Attio: consistent object
- Sana: suggested operational commands
- Raycast / palettes: ASK · SEARCH · NAVIGATE · ACT in one field

Claude’s warm paper canvas is **not** the Control OS canvas. Legacy HTML near-black wins. Claude still informs *restraint*.

### Also rejected

- CRM / ERP / analytics navigation
- Permanent inspector
- Engine names in the rail
- ChatGPT white void as the OS
- 64px TWIN / CAUSE / TIME / ACT rail (legacy HTML chrome)
